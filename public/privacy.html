Privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Publisher: Idan Robbins

PoKeyBoard is a piano and music-practice app. You can use it without creating an account. Your performances are saved on your device; PoKeyBoard does not run an account service, cloud library, advertising service, or app analytics service.

Your music and settings

Recordings contain the notes you play, their timing and velocity, pedal events, and the title and settings you choose. The app stores recordings, practice progress, preferences, and cached exports in your browser profile. Downloaded piano sounds and app files are cached for offline use. Recording uses note events, not your microphone.

Files you select for import are read locally. Audio and sheet-music exports are generated locally. PoKeyBoard does not automatically upload your recordings or imported files.

Network requests and hosting

The app downloads its code, piano samples, and bundled scores from GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including when you are not signed in. Its handling of that information is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you choose to import from a URL, your device contacts that host directly. That host receives the request and normal connection information, such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. Following external links also contacts the linked service.

Device permissions

MIDI and Bluetooth connections are optional. When you enable or connect them, the browser may request permission to access a keyboard. The app uses incoming notes, velocity, pedal and controller messages to play music. Compatible key lights may use outgoing MIDI messages, including optional system-exclusive messages. Device names and connection preferences can be remembered locally. PoKeyBoard does not request microphone, camera, contacts, or location access.

You can disconnect devices in Settings and revoke site permissions through your browser or installed app's permissions controls. Persistent-storage permission helps protect local music from automatic eviction; it does not send music to a server.

Sharing is your choice

Sharing a file sends it to the application or recipient you select. A performance link contains the performance data in the URL fragment. Anyone who receives the full link can open that music and its title; treat the link as a copy of your performance. The fragment is not included in the HTTP request to our hosting service, but the messaging service or recipient you share it with receives the full link.

Keep, export, or delete your data

Use Takes to export individual performances or back up your takes and settings. Delete unwanted takes from the app and remove downloaded piano packs in Settings. To remove all locally stored app data, use your browser or installed app's site-data controls. Clearing site data can also remove practice progress and offline downloads. Uninstalling alone may not clear browser site data.

Your local data stays until you delete it or your browser removes it. Different browser profiles or installations can have separate storage. Back up important music before switching profiles, reinstalling, or clearing storage. Because there is no cloud account, the publisher cannot retrieve or remotely delete your local recordings.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, use the PoKeyBoard issue tracker. Issues are public: do not include private recordings, contact details, credentials, or sensitive information. GitHub handles information you submit there under its own privacy policy.

Changes to these practices will be described here with an updated effective date.
