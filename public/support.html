PoKeyBoard support

Play, learn, record, and share piano music. PoKeyBoard is published by Idan Robbins.

No sound?

Click or tap a piano key to let the browser start audio. Check Piano volume, your Windows output device, and the app volume in the Windows volume mixer. On a first visit, allow the selected piano's sounds to finish downloading.

Using a MIDI keyboard

Connect your keyboard and enable MIDI in Settings → Playing. Approve the browser's permission request. For a compatible Bluetooth MIDI keyboard, use the Bluetooth connection button and choose your device. A hardware keyboard is optional: you can also play using touch, a mouse, or your computer keyboard.

Playing offline

Open the app online once to cache its interface. In Settings → Sound, download the piano you want to use. Wait until it is marked available offline before disconnecting. Bundled scores become available offline after opening them online. Importing a URL and following support links need a connection.

Back up your music

In Takes, use Backup all takes and keep the downloaded JSON file somewhere safe. Restore backup merges those takes back into the app. Your music is local to the browser profile: moving to another browser or installation does not automatically transfer it. Back up before clearing app data or reinstalling.

Updates

The app checks for web updates in the background. Apply an available update from Settings → App when you have finished recording or playing. Windows package updates, when needed, are delivered through Microsoft Store.

Report a problem

Search existing issues or report a problem on GitHub. Reading issues does not require an account; posting does.

  • Include the version and build shown in About or Settings → App.
  • Describe what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
  • Include your Windows/browser version and keyboard model if relevant.
  • Use a small non-private example when reporting an import or playback issue.

Issues are public. Do not post passwords, personal contact details, private recordings, or sensitive diagnostic information. The publisher cannot recover recordings that have been deleted from your device without a backup.
